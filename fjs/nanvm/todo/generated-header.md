## The fixture generators bypass `generatedHeader`

**Priority:** P5
**Status:** open

### Problem

[`fjs/media/rust/item`](../../media/rust/item/module.f.js)'s
`generatedHeader` is the one `// @generated by …. Do not edit: ….` line,
and [`demos`](../demos/module.f.mjs), [`harness`](../harness/module.f.mjs),
[`methods`](../methods/module.f.mjs), [`commands`](../commands/module.f.mjs)
and [`rust`](../rust/module.f.mjs) write their headers through it. The
fixture generators do not: `generate` in each of
[`fast/deferred_owning_tail_calls`](../fast/deferred_owning_tail_calls/module.f.mjs),
[`fast/independent_tail_recipes`](../fast/independent_tail_recipes/module.f.mjs),
[`fast/mixed_self_uses`](../fast/mixed_self_uses/module.f.mjs),
[`fast/immutable_joins`](../fast/immutable_joins/module.f.mjs) and
[`values`](../values/module.f.mjs) writes a `// Fixture source: …;
regenerate with …` line of its own, and `header` in
[`expected`](../expected/module.f.mjs) a third wording, so the generated
Rust under `nanvm/harness/gen.fast` and `gen.values` is the only generated
Rust that does not say `@generated`. Three of the five also copy the same
post-pass:

```js
].join('\n').split('\n').map(line => line.trimEnd()).join('\n')
```

which trims the trailing spaces `generateRust` in
[`fjs/compiler/rust`](../../compiler/rust/module.f.mjs) leaves on blank
lines; the fourth trims inside its own indentation loop.

### Proposal

Every header through `generatedHeader`, and the trim in the one place that
produces the whitespace, `generateRust`, or failing that a `trimLines`
beside `indent` in `media/rust/item`. A `fixtureModule(source, fast)` in
`nanvm/fast` then makes three of the five generators one line each.
