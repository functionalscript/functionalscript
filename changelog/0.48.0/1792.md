- `ci`: the `wasm` job runs in a generated Nix flake instead of installing
  `dtolnay/rust-toolchain`, `bytecodealliance/actions/wasmtime/setup` and
  `wasmerio/setup-wasmer`. Its toolchain comes from a second flake input,
  `oxalica/rust-overlay`, because Nixpkgs builds no standard library for three
  of the job's four WASM targets; Wasmtime and Wasmer come from the pinned
  Nixpkgs snapshot, and the job asserts both versions from inside its shell.
