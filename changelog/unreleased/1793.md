- `ci`: the `bun` job runs in a generated Nix flake instead of installing
  `oven-sh/setup-bun`. Nixpkgs ships a Bun two of this repository's proofs fail
  on, so the flake keeps that package's recipe and overrides the archive it
  unpacks with an exact upstream release, pinned by a hash in
  `fjs/ci/config/module.f.mjs`; the job asserts the resulting version from
  inside its shell.
