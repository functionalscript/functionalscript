- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `nanvm`: the shared operator corpus reuses the
  canonical EDAG operation ids and runs the expression each case denotes. `Op`
  is gone — a group carries an `Op1Id` or an `Op2Id` — and `rust`'s `call`
  gives way to `nodeExpr`.
- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `nanvm`: `Case` takes the operand count its id fixes,
  so a bare `Case` — or `Special`, now generic — no longer resolves, and
  `Value` splits into `Value`, `Operand`, and `Expectation`, each thunk legal
  in one position only.
