- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `fjs/ci/nix`: `nixDevelopAll` and `nixVersionCheckStep`
  are removed, and with them the generated `nix-flakes` CI job. CI uses the
  generated flakes rather than checking them; what they declare is asserted by
  proofs over the generator's output.
- `ci`: the Node 24 job runs `npm ci` and `node --test` through its generated
  Nix flake, one step each, instead of installing Node with `setup-node`.
