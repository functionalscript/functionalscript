- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `edag`: a call now carries the chain it is made from.
  `['()', exp, lambda, exp]` is its own node, so `()` is no longer an `op2` id,
  and `propertyCall` (`['.()', …]`) is gone — `a.b(...c)` is
  `['()', a, [['|.', 'b']], c]`, an ordinary call `['()', f, [], c]`.
- `edag`: optional chaining — new `['?.', exp, index, lambda]` and
  `['?.()', exp, lambda, exp, lambda]` nodes, plus the four chain steps
  `['|.', index]`, `['|()', exp]`, `['|?.', index]`, and `['|?.()', exp]`
  carrying receiver and short-circuit control flow.
