- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `edag`: a call now carries the chain it is made from.
  `['()', exp, lambdas, exp]` is its own node, so `()` is no longer an `op2`
  id and `propertyCall` (`['.()', …]`) is gone.
- `edag`: optional chaining — new `?.` and `?.()` nodes, plus the chain steps
  `|.`, `|()`, `|?.`, and `|?.()` that carry receiver and short-circuit
  control flow.
