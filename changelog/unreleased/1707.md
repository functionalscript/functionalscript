- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `edag`: the chain nodes are restructured. `()` is
  `['()', exp, exp]` and no longer carries a `lambdas`, `?.` is
  `['?.', exp, index]`, `?.()` is `['?.()', exp, exp]`, and the new `.()`
  (`['.()', exp, index, exp]`) spells a method call. Every importer of
  `PropertyAccessor`, `OptionalPropertyAccessor`, or `OptionalCall` moves to
  `Dot`, `OptionDot`, or `OptionCall`.
- `edag`: new `_` and `_()` nodes, the two walkers that carry a `lambdas` —
  now only where an optional region reaches past its own operands.
- `edag/canonical`: new `canonical(exp)`, which checks the cardinality and
  minimality conditions on `_`/`_()` that `validate` cannot state.
