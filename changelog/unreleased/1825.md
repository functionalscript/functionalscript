- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `ci-update`, `dev-update`, and `update` are gone.
  `gen` (deterministic, Nix-independent) covers what `ci-update`/`dev-update`
  did; `lock-update` (needs Nix) covers what `update` did, plus refreshing
  every generated `flake.lock` and `Cargo.lock`.
- `ci`: `flake.lock` is no longer generated from pinned `narHash`/
  `lastModified` data; `fjs ci` now also writes `nix/lock-update.sh`, one
  `nix flake lock` per generated flake, for `npm run lock-update` to run.
