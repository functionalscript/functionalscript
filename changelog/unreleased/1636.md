- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `effects`: `IoEffect` is renamed `Effect`, with `E`
  defaulting to `NotImplemented`, and the `Pure | Do` representation is renamed
  `RawEffect`. Both stay public: `Effect` for what can fail, `RawEffect` for
  what cannot — a `List` cell, a `Program`'s exit code, a tool result.
- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `effects`: `okStep` is removed. It was a step adapter
  whose only caller was the Io `step`, which now writes that branch itself.
