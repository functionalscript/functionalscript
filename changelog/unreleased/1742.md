- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `emergent_testing`: the browser runner awaits only
  `instanceof Promise` values, as `fjs t` does. A promise from another realm is
  walked as a proof tree rather than awaited, the gap `fjs t` has had all along.
  A same-realm promise is awaited, and `await` — unlike `then` — is not
  diverted by the value's own `then` or by a custom `Symbol.species` that is a
  valid constructor. A species that throws, or that is not a constructor, is
  reported as that test's failure, as `fjs t` reports it
