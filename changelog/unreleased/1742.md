- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `emergent_testing`: the browser runner awaits only
  `instanceof Promise` values, as `fjs t` does. A promise from another realm is
  walked as a proof tree rather than awaited, the gap `fjs t` has had all along.
  A same-realm promise is settled through the intrinsic `Promise.prototype.then`,
  so the value's own `then` and a custom `Symbol.species` cannot divert it, and
  a promise whose `constructor` has been replaced still settles. A species that
  throws, or that is not a constructor, is reported as that test's failure
