- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `cas`: `Cas.list` answers `IoResult`. A store that
  exists and cannot be walked is now an `error` the caller handles, where it
  used to `throw`; an absent store still answers `ok([])`.
- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `cas/evo`: `Evo.list` and `Evo.head` are fallible, and
  `Evo.add` nests its domain `Result` inside the effect channel. A cache slot
  the runner cannot reach becomes an MCP `isError` result instead of a panic.
