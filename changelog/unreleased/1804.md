  publishing workflow that until now was hand-written and had drifted from
  every convention the generated one follows — it takes its runner image, Node
  version and pinned action refs from `ci/config` like any other generated job,
  and keeps the publish intent it had: a push to `main`, `contents: read` with
  `id-token: write`, and `npm publish --provenance`

  workflow schema's `on` admits `push` with its branch list
