- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `emergent_testing`: the proof traversal is sequential —
  one leaf, its report included, finishes before the next starts.
  `runModuleMap` performs no `all`, so a proof relying on sibling concurrency
  now deadlocks, and `fjs t` times each leaf alone
- `effects`: `foldStep` and `forEachStep` no longer nest a continuation per
  item, which cost quadratic time and failed above ~10,000 items. Order,
  short-circuiting and signatures are unchanged
- `effects`: new `walkStep`, a `foldStep` whose body can add items, walked
  before the ones that remain — a depth-first walk that costs no continuation
  per level
