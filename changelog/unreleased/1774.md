- **BREAKING CHANGES:** `emergent_testing`: the shared proof traversal runs
  **sequentially**. One leaf's whole chain — its call, its report, and the tree
  its return value produced — finishes before the next leaf starts, for sibling
  leaves and for modules alike. `runModule` and `runModuleMap` therefore
  perform no `all`, and their operation sets drop it: a runner handed to them
  needs no `all` handler, while a proof that depended on a sibling running
  concurrently now deadlocks instead of passing. `fjs t` is the observable
  half — every line lands as its test finishes, in structural order, and a
  leaf's reported duration is its own time. That last part was not true
  before: under the fan-out each leaf was charged with whatever its siblings
  did while it was suspended, so this repository's own suite reported 754 s of
  test time for a run that took 61.6 s, and named a 1.4 ms proof as 17.6 s.
  Sequentially the same suite reports 63.4 s for a 65.2 s run — the summed
  durations and the wall clock agree, and the ~6% of wall clock the fan-out
  saved buys nothing a proof runner needs. The browser page keeps its own
  scheduling until it moves onto this traversal
