- `effects`: `Import`, `Module` and `import_` moved from `effects/node` to
  `effects/common`, where operations with more than one implementer live — a
  browser page loads modules too. `effects/node` re-exports all three, so
  importing them through it is unchanged
- `effects`: `Import` is now a `readonly` tuple, like every operation beside it.
  Reading it is unaffected; assigning one into a mutable-tuple slot no longer
  type-checks
