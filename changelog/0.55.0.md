- `compiler`: accepts `String(x)` and `String()`; `String` is a reserved
  global conversion name, so bindings named `String` must be renamed, while
  property names such as `{ String: 1 }.String` stay valid. (#2763)
- `compiler`: accepts untagged template literals, nested templates and
  substitutions included: each `${e}` lowers to `String(e)` and the chunks
  join with `+`, so the EDAG equals the explicit concatenation. Chunks admit
  the common string escapes plus an escaped backtick and `\${`; tagged
  templates and other escapes are refused. `js/tokenizer` and `ebnf/lib/js`
  emit template chunk tokens with a cooked `value` and the exact `source`.
  (#2823)

  `[text, kind]` with kinds `keyword`, `literal`, `string`, `number`,
  `comment`, `operator` and `identifier`; `toText`, `fromSpans`,
  `withNames`, `tagged`, `fromTagged`, `untagged`, `textOfResult` and the
  `Chunk` helpers `chunkText`, `chunkRun`, `chunksMarked`, `chunksText` and
  `chunkStrings`. Names are ASCII. (#2726, #2729, #2753, #2760, #2818)

  `nullSerialize` and `boolSerialize` answer runs instead of plain strings;

  `treeSerialize` and `LeafSerializer` are typed on `List<Chunk>`, as are
  `media/datajs/serializer`'s `leafSerialize` and `keySerialize` and
  `compiler/serializer`'s `Document`. Join chunks with `chunksText` from
  `text/marked`. The chunks `trySerialize` and `tryModuleSerialize` answer
  split keywords from their surroundings; every output's joined text is
  unchanged. (#2729, #2753)

  curves with a prime `n` and `n² > p + 1 + 2√p`. `sign` now reduces `r`
